Knit One, Chain One

Privacy Policy

Knit One, Chain One is built to keep your information private. In short: everything you put in the app stays on your own phone. There are no accounts, and nothing is sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in and no account, and it does not send your patterns, projects, photos or any other information to us or to any server. We have no way to see what you keep in the app.

Where your information is kept

Your patterns, projects, stash, shopping list, settings, photos and any imported PDFs are stored on your phone, inside the app. They stay there unless you remove them or uninstall the app.

Photos and the camera

The app asks to use your camera and photos only so you can add pictures to your own patterns, projects and stash. Those photos are kept in the app on your phone. If you’d prefer the photos you take not to also be saved to your phone’s gallery, turn on “Hide photos from your phone” in Settings.

Notifications

If you turn on Motivational Reminders, the reminders are created and shown by your phone on its own. They are not sent from any server, and no information leaves your device to make them work.

Backups

When you make a backup, the app writes a file on your phone and then opens your phone’s share menu so you can choose where to keep it — for example Google Drive, email, or Files. The app does not upload your backup anywhere itself; where it goes is entirely your choice, and from that point it is handled by whichever service you pick.

Purchases

If you buy Premium, the purchase is handled by Google Play. Payment is processed by Google, not by this app, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only records on your phone that Premium is unlocked.

Children

The app is a general-audience tool for knitting and crochet. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear here and, where required, on the app’s store listing, with a new date above.

Contact

If you have any questions about your privacy or this policy, you can reach us at razzleedesignsco@gmail.com.
